# Cover specification (draft, numbers unverified)

Date: 2026-10-13. Page count is fixed at 614 (owner accepted). Trim 5.5 x 8.5 in, cream paper.

## Estimated KDP paperback wrap

- Spine: 614 pages x 0.0025 in = **1.535 in** (KDP cream-paper factor, from memory).
- Bleed: 0.125 in on all outer edges.
- Full wrap width: 0.125 + 5.5 + 1.535 + 5.5 + 0.125 = **12.785 in**.
- Full wrap height: 0.125 + 8.5 + 0.125 = **8.75 in**.
- Spine text is allowed above 100 pages. Keep text 0.125 in clear of spine edges.
- Barcode area: about 2 x 1.2 in on the back cover, bottom right. KDP adds the ISBN barcode itself.

## Must verify before art is made

1. Use KDP's cover calculator with 614 pages, cream paper, 5.5 x 8.5. Use its numbers if they differ.
2. IngramSpark has its own template and paper options. Spine width differs by paper stock. Get its template for the same page count.
3. Real ISBNs are needed for the barcode.

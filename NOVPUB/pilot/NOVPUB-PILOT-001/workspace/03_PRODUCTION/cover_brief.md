# Cover brief

Title: THE BLUE SILENCE
Author: Darya Malak
Tagline: Everyone knows the police protect the law. Nobody asks who protects the police from themselves.

## Direction
- Deep blue palette. One institutional corridor or building. No gun, no blood.
- Large title, author name small.
- Must read as a Malaysian crime thriller at thumbnail size.

## Production route
- AI-assisted art. Disclose on upload to KDP and IngramSpark.
- Check both platforms' current AI-content rules before upload.
- Keep prompts and source images as evidence in the pilot folder.

## Specs (to verify against each platform's current template)
- Paperback trim: 5.5 x 8.5 in, cream paper.
- Print cover needs bleed and a spine. Spine width depends on the final page count, so the print cover is finalised after the interior is built.
- Ebook cover: portrait, height to width about 1.6 to 1. Confirm the platform's recommended pixel size.
- Both versions need a clear title at small size.

## Approval
- Cover files and the physical proof are approved by the owner. No AI approval.

# Human Approval Policy

Human approval is mandatory when a decision materially changes literary content, ownership/rights, publication identity, ISBN assignment, commercial exposure, physical product acceptance, distribution, platform exclusivity, review integrity, or live publication state.

An approval record must contain:

- `approval_id`
- subject/gate/action
- exact artefact or decision version
- approver identity/reference
- timestamp
- decision (`approved`, `rejected`, `conditional`)
- conditions/notes
- evidence references

An agent recommendation is not an approval.

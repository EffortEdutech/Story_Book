# Phase 7 — Launch & Marketing Architecture

Phase 7 is a governed launch operating layer, not an unbounded marketing generator. All public facts resolve to approved NOVPUB sources. ARC and reviewer workflows preserve reviewer independence. Amazon customer-review rules are adapter snapshots refreshed before live outreach. GATE-09 requires current proof/channel readiness, launch assets, validated links, review-policy compliance and explicit human launch authorization.

## Source hierarchy
Approved manuscript/rights/production/metadata/channel state → launch claims registry → campaign assets → public execution.

## Core invariants
- ARC provision is never conditioned on a review or a positive/star-specific review.
- Close personal/financial relationships do not become Amazon customer-review targets.
- Manuscript quotes are exact and traceable.
- Bestseller/award/media/distribution claims require evidence.
- Retail availability never becomes a claim of physical bookstore stocking without proof.
- Rank is not sales volume.
- Launch activation and spend remain human-authorized actions.

# AFTER IGARI - Continuity and Production Control

## Freeze-Lock Principle

Once a structural decision is frozen, manuscript production must preserve it unless a genuine contradiction requires correction.

Revision may improve prose, pacing, clarity, subtlety, and continuity, but it must not redesign the story's purpose.

## Production Order

1. Apply the Master Manuscript Freeze-Lock before Part I.
2. Apply the relevant Part Production Prompt.
3. Draft one chapter at a time.
4. After each chapter, run a brief continuity check.
5. Use historical fact-checking whenever real-world specifics appear.
6. After Chapters 9, 18, 29, and 36, run a formal Part Continuity & Quality Gate.
7. After Chapter 36, run the five-pass whole-manuscript review.
8. Then run final-line review.
9. Then draft the Author's Note.

## Chapter Preflight

Before drafting a chapter, verify:

- chapter place in the 36-chapter architecture;
- Adam's emotional state entering;
- Adam's emotional state leaving;
- allowed dream/reality level;
- required historical facts;
- continuity of characters, objects, memories, and motifs;
- that no frozen rule is being changed.

## End-of-Part Gate

After each part, audit:

- structure;
- Adam's psychology;
- supporting characters;
- historical boundary;
- style;
- dreams;
- continuity of time, phone/message state, watch behaviour, itinerary, memories, promises, relationships, and cabin positioning.

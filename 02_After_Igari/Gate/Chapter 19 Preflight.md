5. Use hindsight or winks: "last time", "little did he know", "if only", "he would never".
6. Add Hana-message beats, phone checks, watch checks or clock stamps.
7. Add medical exposition, a real hospital, a real doctor or real brands.

9. Turn Nadia into a scold. She is practical and tender in the illness years; keep her human (character doc).

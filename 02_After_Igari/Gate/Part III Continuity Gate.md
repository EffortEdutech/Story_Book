## Findings and rulings (R1–R5)

Each finding is followed by the writer's ruling. The suggested defaults below are the original gate text.

**R1. Ch26 explains its own insight.** This is the one real quality finding. Ch26 is the longest chapter (2,212 words against 1,371–1,990 for the rest) and the three concrete scenes carry it: the bicycle, the plastic horse and the fractions. Between them, Adam says what each means, more than once, and twice remarks on his own tidiness. That conflicts with the Voice Bible ("no explanation of symbolism", "behaviour before explanation") and with the Character doc (Adam must not be too self-aware too early).

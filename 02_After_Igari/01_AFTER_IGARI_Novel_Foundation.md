# AFTER IGARI - Novel Foundation

## Core Shape

AFTER IGARI is a work of literary psychological fiction set inside the emotional shadow of Malaysia Airlines Flight MH370.

The novel must not attempt to solve MH370. It must not propose a cause, perpetrator, route, cockpit event, crash site, rescue, afterlife confirmation, or hidden explanation. Its power comes from remaining inside uncertainty and using a fictional passenger to examine grief, responsibility, presence, memory, and love.

## Governing Promise

The novel uses a real unresolved event as historical boundary, not as puzzle machinery.

The reader may know MH370 history, but Adam Idris does not receive omniscient knowledge. The manuscript stays close to his limited perception. When history enters, it enters through verifiable chronology, not speculation.

## Genre Position

- Literary psychological fiction.
- Family drama under extreme uncertainty.
- Restrained spiritual and emotional reckoning.
- Not a thriller.
- Not a mystery solution.
- Not aviation reconstruction.
- Not conspiracy fiction.

## Central Character

Adam Idris is fictional. His family is fictional. His passenger experience is fictional. He must not be written as a stand-in for any real person aboard MH370.

Adam begins as a man who believes responsibility means providing, fixing, planning, and returning later. The novel gradually strips that logic down until he understands presence as something more fragile and more necessary.

## Emotional Engine

Adam's unopened message from Hana is the central emotional pressure.

The story is not driven by external revelation. It is driven by Adam's growing inability to postpone love, apology, fear, faith, and memory.

## Ethical Boundary

The 239 real people aboard MH370 must be treated with dignity. The novel must avoid sensationalism, imitation of real passengers, and invented certainty. The Author's Note must clearly separate fiction from historical fact.

## Title Logic

IGARI marks a real navigational point in the known MH370 chronology. In the novel, it also becomes the symbolic threshold after which ordinary time, certainty, and Adam's old form of control begin to fail.

The title should not imply that the novel knows what happened after IGARI. It means the novel lives inside the human and moral uncertainty that follows.

# AFTER IGARI - Historical and World-Building Research Ledger

## Historical Boundary

AFTER IGARI uses verified historical chronology where appropriate, but it must not reconstruct unknown events aboard MH370.

The manuscript may use public, authoritative facts as frame. It must soften or remove details that cannot be verified and are not necessary.

## Key Known Chronology for Part I

- Malaysia Airlines Flight MH370 was a real flight.
- The aircraft departed Kuala Lumpur for Beijing.
- Part I preserves takeoff as ordinary.
- Chapter 5 preserves 00:42 as takeoff timing.
- The final recorded radio acknowledgement occurred at 01:19:30 MYT.
- The official investigation records MH370 passing IGARI at 01:20:31 MYT.

## Adam's Knowledge Rule

Adam does not know the historical meaning of 01:19 or IGARI while experiencing Chapter 8 and Chapter 9.

The narration must not give him information from official reports, later news, radar interpretation, or hindsight.

## Fact Labels

Use these labels when reviewing any chapter that touches real-world specifics:

- H1 - verified historical fact;
- H2 - reasonable passenger inference;
- F1 - fictional Adam/family material;
- D1 - dream or perception;
- O1 - unresolved or deliberately ambiguous.

## Required Fact-Check Areas

Run a focused fact check when a chapter materially includes:

- MH370 chronology;
- KLIA details;
- aircraft/cabin details;
- aviation procedure;
- astronomy;
- 2014 phone or message technology;
- Islamic practice;
- Malaysian geography;
- real locations and travel timings.

## Research Discipline

Do not add researched information merely because it is interesting.

If a detail cannot be verified and is unnecessary, make the prose less specific rather than inventing certainty.

The historical/fiction boundary is more important than technical density.

## Real-Person Protection

Adam Idris, Nadia, Hana, Rahman, Salmah, and all fictional passenger experiences must not be understood as representing real passengers, crew, or families.

Avoid biographical overlaps with real passengers whenever possible.

# The actual prompt sequence I recommend
You do not need to write a huge instruction every single time. Use this production order:
1. Once before Part I: paste the Master Freeze-Lock Prompt, then the Part I prompt.
2. For every chapter: use the Standard Chapter-Drafting Prompt with the chapter number/title.
3. After Chapters 9, 18, 29 and 36: run the End-of-Part Review Prompt.
4. Use the Historical Fact-Check Prompt whenever a chapter relies on real-world specifics.
5. If we resume in another conversation, start with the Master Freeze-Lock + Continuity Recovery prompts.
6. After Chapter 36: run the five-pass Whole-Manuscript Review, then Final-Line Review, and finally the Author’s Note.

So when we are ready to start, your very next manuscript prompt can simply be:
Proceed with manuscript production: Chapter 1 — The Message. Bismillah. Apply the complete AFTER IGARI frozen canon, Master Manuscript Freeze-Lock, and Part I Production Prompt. Write the complete polished chapter and do not proceed to Chapter 2.

That system should keep the 36-chapter manuscript tightly inside everything we have frozen while still leaving enough creative freedom for the prose itself.


# A. STANDARD CHAPTER-DRAFTING PROMPT

Use this for every chapter.

Replace the bracketed fields only.

---

Proceed with manuscript production:

# Chapter [NUMBER] — [TITLE]

Bismillah.

Apply:

- the complete AFTER IGARI frozen canon;
- the Master Manuscript Freeze-Lock;
- the relevant Part Production Prompt;
- every continuity decision established in all previously completed chapters.

Before drafting, internally verify:

1. the chapter’s place in the frozen 36-chapter architecture;
2. Adam’s emotional state entering the chapter;
3. Adam’s emotional state leaving the chapter;
4. the allowed dream/reality level at this stage;
5. historical facts required for this chapter;
6. continuity with every established character, object, memory and motif;
7. that no frozen rule is being unintentionally changed.

Then write the **complete polished manuscript chapter**, not an outline, summary or commentary.

Requirements:

- deep third-person limited entirely through Adam;
- past tense;
- literary but accessible;
- emotionally restrained;
- Malaysian identity natural rather than explained;
- selective Malay where appropriate;
- Islamic vocabulary quiet and authentic;
- behaviour before explanation;
- sensory grounding;
- no melodrama;
- no omniscient MH370 information;
- no invented historical certainty;
- no new conspiracy or solution;
- no unnecessary new characters or subplot;
- no authorial foreshadowing about the historical fate of MH370;
- no “little did he know” or “last time” language;
- dream typography identical to waking prose;
- maintain the established motif frequency without overusing it;
- do not summarise previous chapters unnecessarily;
- do not explain symbolism.

Target length should follow the architecture naturally rather than padding the chapter.

After the manuscript chapter, add only a short **Continuity Check** confirming:

- frozen architecture preserved;
- historical boundary preserved;
- character continuity preserved;
- emotional objective achieved;
- any genuine unresolved continuity issue discovered.

Do not propose redesigns unless an actual contradiction is discovered.

Do not begin the next chapter.

# C. END-OF-PART REVIEW PROMPT

Use after Chapter 9, Chapter 18, Chapter 29 and Chapter 36.

---

We have completed **PART [NUMBER] — [PART TITLE]** of AFTER IGARI.

Before proceeding to the next Part, perform a formal **Part Continuity & Quality Gate**.

Do not rewrite the entire Part unless necessary.

Audit all chapters in this Part against the frozen canon.

Check:

## Structure
- Did every chapter perform its frozen narrative function?
- Did the emotional escalation occur at the intended pace?
- Was anything revealed too early or too late?

## Adam
- Is his psychology consistent?
- Is his transformation gradual?
- Does he remain sympathetic and recognisable?
- Have we accidentally made him too selfish, too saintly or too self-aware too soon?

## Supporting characters
- Is Nadia fully human rather than merely corrective?
- Is Hana consistently eleven?
- Is Rahman restrained and ordinary?
- Is Salmah natural and non-symbolic?

## Historical boundary
- Are MH370 facts correct?
- Did we introduce anything Adam could not know?
- Did we imply any unsupported cause, route, cockpit event or passenger behaviour?
- Did any fictional supporting character risk resembling a specific real passenger?

## Style
- Is the prose literary but accessible?
- Are emotional scenes understated?
- Is Malaysian identity organic?
- Are Malay/Islamic terms natural?
- Have motifs been overused?
- Are there clichés or authorial winks?

## Dreams
- Is the dream escalation appropriate for this Part?
- Has ambiguity increased without becoming confusion?
- Does every surreal event have emotional purpose?

## Continuity
- times;
- phone/message state;
- watch behaviour;
- work itinerary;
- family memories;
- return itinerary;
- Sunday/Saturn promise;
- character relationships;
- cabin positioning;
- previous dialogue references.

Give me:

1. **PASS / PASS WITH CORRECTIONS / HOLD**
2. any corrections required before the next Part;
3. a concise Continuity Ledger Update;
4. confirmation of what must remain unchanged going into the next Part.

Do not begin the next Part yet.

# D. HISTORICAL FACT-CHECK PROMPT

Use whenever a chapter includes specific real-world MH370, KLIA, aviation, astronomy, 2014 technology, Islamic-practice or geographic details.

---

Before finalising Chapter [NUMBER], perform a focused historical and world-building fact check.

Check only facts that materially appear in the chapter.

Classify each relevant detail internally as:

- H1 — verified historical fact;
- H2 — reasonable passenger inference;
- F1 — fictional Adam material;
- D1 — dream/perception;
- O1 — unresolved.

Verify any H1 claim using authoritative or strong period sources where necessary.

Do not add researched information to the chapter merely because it is interesting.

If a detail cannot be verified and is unnecessary, make the prose less specific rather than inventing certainty.

Protect the historical/fiction boundary above all.

Then return:

- any required factual correction;
- any wording that should remain deliberately ambiguous;
- whether the chapter is cleared for manuscript use.

# H. AUTHOR’S NOTE PROMPT

Use only after the manuscript itself is frozen.

---

Draft the final **Author’s Note** for AFTER IGARI.

It must be concise, dignified and explicit about the historical-fiction boundary.

It should state that:

- Malaysia Airlines Flight MH370 was real;
- 239 people were aboard;
- Adam Idris, his family and the novel’s fictional passenger experiences are inventions;
- the novel uses verified historical chronology where appropriate;
- imagined scenes do not claim to reconstruct what actually occurred aboard MH370;
- the novel does not propose a solution to the disappearance;
- real passengers, crew and their families should not be understood as represented by Adam or the fictional supporting characters.

Tone:

respectful, restrained, non-defensive and non-sensational.

Do not discuss conspiracy theories.

Do not explain the novel’s ending.

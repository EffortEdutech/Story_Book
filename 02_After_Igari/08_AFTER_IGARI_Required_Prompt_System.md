# AFTER IGARI - Required Prompt System

## Production Sequence

1. Once before Part I: apply the Master Freeze-Lock Prompt, then the Part I prompt.
2. For every chapter: use the Standard Chapter-Drafting Prompt with chapter number and title.
3. After Chapters 9, 18, 29, and 36: run the End-of-Part Review Prompt.
4. Use the Historical Fact-Check Prompt whenever a chapter relies on real-world specifics.
5. If resuming in another conversation, start with Master Freeze-Lock plus Continuity Recovery.
6. After Chapter 36: run the five-pass Whole-Manuscript Review, then Final-Line Review, then Author's Note.

## Standard Chapter-Drafting Prompt

Proceed with manuscript production:

# Chapter [NUMBER] - [TITLE]

Bismillah.

Apply:

- the complete AFTER IGARI frozen canon;
- the Master Manuscript Freeze-Lock;
- the relevant Part Production Prompt;
- every continuity decision established in all previously completed chapters.

Before drafting, internally verify:

1. the chapter's place in the frozen 36-chapter architecture;
2. Adam's emotional state entering the chapter;
3. Adam's emotional state leaving the chapter;
4. the allowed dream/reality level at this stage;
5. historical facts required for this chapter;
6. continuity with every established character, object, memory and motif;
7. that no frozen rule is being unintentionally changed.

Then write the complete polished manuscript chapter, not an outline, summary or commentary.

Requirements:

- deep third-person limited entirely through Adam;
- past tense;
- literary but accessible;
- emotionally restrained;
- Malaysian identity natural rather than explained;
- selective Malay where appropriate;
- Islamic vocabulary quiet and authentic;
- behaviour before explanation;
- sensory grounding;
- no melodrama;
- no omniscient MH370 information;
- no invented historical certainty;
- no new conspiracy or solution;
- no unnecessary new characters or subplot;
- no authorial foreshadowing about the historical fate of MH370;
- no "little did he know" or "last time" language;
- dream typography identical to waking prose;
- maintain motif frequency without overusing it;
- do not summarise previous chapters unnecessarily;
- do not explain symbolism.

After the manuscript chapter, add only a short Continuity Check confirming:

- frozen architecture preserved;
- historical boundary preserved;
- character continuity preserved;
- emotional objective achieved;
- any genuine unresolved continuity issue discovered.

Do not begin the next chapter.

## Chapter Revision Prompt

Revise Chapter [NUMBER] - [TITLE] of AFTER IGARI.

Bismillah.

Preserve the complete frozen canon and all established continuity.

Do not redesign the plot or change the chapter's structural function.

Review the chapter against:

- Novel Foundation;
- Character & Emotional Architecture;
- Narrative & Chapter Architecture;
- Voice, Style & Manuscript Rules Bible;
- Historical & World-Building Research Ledger;
- all previous manuscript chapters.

Strengthen where needed:

- Adam's POV;
- emotional subtlety;
- sensory reality;
- Malaysian authenticity;
- dialogue naturalness;
- pacing;
- memory transitions;
- dream ambiguity;
- motif control;
- historical accuracy;
- chapter opening;
- chapter ending.

Remove:

- melodrama;
- exposition;
- cliche;
- unnecessary metaphor;
- authorial explanation;
- omniscient information;
- repeated emotional statements;
- overuse of "later," "home," "tomorrow," or similar motifs;
- anything that implies a factual explanation for MH370.

Return the complete revised chapter.

Do not merely describe the edits.

After the chapter, provide a very short revision note listing only substantive continuity changes, if any.

## End-of-Part Review Prompt

We have completed PART [NUMBER] - [PART TITLE] of AFTER IGARI.

Before proceeding to the next Part, perform a formal Part Continuity & Quality Gate.

Do not rewrite the entire Part unless necessary.

Audit all chapters in this Part against the frozen canon.

Check structure, Adam, supporting characters, historical boundary, style, dreams, and continuity.

Give:

1. PASS / PASS WITH CORRECTIONS / HOLD;
2. any corrections required before the next Part;
3. a concise Continuity Ledger Update;
4. confirmation of what must remain unchanged going into the next Part.

Do not begin the next Part yet.

## Historical Fact-Check Prompt

Before finalising Chapter [NUMBER], perform a focused historical and world-building fact check.

Check only facts that materially appear in the chapter.

Classify each relevant detail internally as:

- H1 - verified historical fact;
- H2 - reasonable passenger inference;
- F1 - fictional Adam material;
- D1 - dream/perception;
- O1 - unresolved.

Verify any H1 claim using authoritative or strong period sources where necessary.

Do not add researched information to the chapter merely because it is interesting.

If a detail cannot be verified and is unnecessary, make the prose less specific rather than inventing certainty.

Protect the historical/fiction boundary above all.

Return:

- any required factual correction;
- any wording that should remain deliberately ambiguous;
- whether the chapter is cleared for manuscript use.

## Continuity Recovery Prompt

We are resuming AFTER IGARI manuscript production after a break.

Do not draft new prose yet.

Re-establish the manuscript state from the frozen canon and all completed chapters available to you.

Give a concise Continuity Recovery containing:

- last completed chapter;
- Adam's current physical situation;
- Adam's current emotional state;
- last known state of Hana's voice message;
- current dream/reality escalation level;
- unresolved memories still withheld;
- motifs currently active;
- historical time status;
- any continuity issues requiring attention;
- the exact next chapter and its frozen purpose.

Do not redesign the novel.

Do not speculate about future changes.

Once continuity is restored, wait for the chapter-drafting instruction.

## Final Whole-Manuscript Review Prompt

The complete first manuscript of AFTER IGARI is now drafted.

Do not alter the ending architecture or reopen frozen decisions unless a genuine contradiction requires correction.

Perform five separate audits:

1. Literary Continuity.
2. Character & Emotional Continuity.
3. MH370 Historical Fact Audit.
4. Malaysian & Islamic Authenticity Audit.
5. Ethical Sensitivity Audit.

For each pass provide:

- PASS / PASS WITH CORRECTIONS / HOLD;
- chapter references requiring work;
- exact nature of correction;
- whether correction affects prose only or frozen continuity.

After all five passes, provide a Manuscript Readiness Verdict.

Do not create an epilogue.

Do not explain what happened to MH370.

Do not remove the final ambiguity.

## Final Line Review Prompt

Now review only the final pages of Chapter 36 - Almost Home.

Do not change the ending architecture.

Locked:

- Adam's physical fate unknown;
- no crash;
- no rescue;
- no afterlife confirmation;
- no location confirmation;
- dawn/light;
- Hana/"Abah" ambiguity;
- Adam's final physical stillness.

Evaluate whether the exact final paragraph and final sentence achieve simplicity, emotional resonance, ambiguity, restraint, connection to the opening, and no explanation after the emotional ending.

If the existing final line is already strongest, retain it.

If improvement is genuinely needed, provide no more than three alternatives and explain the tonal difference briefly.

Do not turn the ending into a twist.

## Author's Note Prompt

Draft the final Author's Note for AFTER IGARI.

It must be concise, dignified and explicit about the historical-fiction boundary.

It should state that:

- Malaysia Airlines Flight MH370 was real;
- 239 people were aboard;
- Adam Idris, his family and the novel's fictional passenger experiences are inventions;
- the novel uses verified historical chronology where appropriate;
- imagined scenes do not claim to reconstruct what actually occurred aboard MH370;
- the novel does not propose a solution to the disappearance;
- real passengers, crew and their families should not be understood as represented by Adam or the fictional supporting characters.

Tone: respectful, restrained, non-defensive and non-sensational.

Do not discuss conspiracy theories.

Do not explain the novel's ending.

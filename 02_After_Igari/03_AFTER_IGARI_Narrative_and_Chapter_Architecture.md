# AFTER IGARI - Narrative and Chapter Architecture

## Overall Architecture

The manuscript is frozen as a 36-chapter novel in four parts.

Known part gates:

- Chapter 9 closes Part I - DEPARTURE.
- Chapter 18 closes Part II.
- Chapter 29 closes Part III.
- Chapter 36 closes the manuscript.

Each part must end with a formal continuity and quality gate before proceeding.

## Part I - DEPARTURE

Function: establish ordinary life, Adam's emotional flaw, the family wound, the flight, and the threshold at IGARI without giving Adam any objective knowledge of historical significance.

## Locked Part I Progression

Chapter 5 - Takeoff:

- takeoff remains ordinary;
- 00:42 is preserved;
- Hana's stars question and Adam's "I'll tell you tomorrow" promise are introduced;
- Saturn and the Sunday telescope promise remain unrevealed;
- Hana's voice message remains unopened;
- no anomaly, cockpit knowledge, or historical hindsight appears.

Chapter 6 - Cruise:

- Salmah and Johor enter through sensory memory;
- the bicycle establishes Rahman's quiet provider model;
- "Baca doa" introduces Adam's faith as familiar habit;
- Adam still interprets fixing/providing as sufficient love;
- Hana's message remains unopened;
- no Sunday/Saturn, later Rahman guilt, dream ambiguity, or post-IGARI material is advanced.

Chapter 7 - Thirty-Five Thousand Feet:

- Adam remains confident in responsibility as a virtue;
- Rahman's amanah lesson strengthens that belief rather than challenging it;
- Hana's message remains unopened;
- later Rahman illness, Sunday/Saturn details, and all post-IGARI uncertainty remain withheld.

Chapter 8 - Good Night:

- the cabin is quiet;
- Adam's early Nadia memory appears without revealing later marriage material;
- his "that was before responsibility" defence is preserved;
- Hana's message remains unopened;
- the historical 01:19 significance stays outside Adam's awareness.

Chapter 9 - IGARI:

- official chronology places MH370 passing IGARI at 01:20:31 MYT, after the final recorded radio acknowledgement at 01:19:30 MYT;
- Adam is not told this;
- he experiences only uncertain physical sensation;
- the ordinary world remains intact around him;
- Hana's message remains unheard;
- no objective turn is asserted;
- uncertainty has only just begun.

## Manuscript Ending Lock

Chapter 36 - Almost Home must preserve:

- Adam's physical fate unknown;
- no crash;
- no rescue;
- no afterlife confirmation;
- no location confirmation;
- dawn/light;
- Hana/"Abah" ambiguity;
- Adam's final physical stillness.

The ending must not become a twist or explanation.

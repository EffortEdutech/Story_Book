# AFTER IGARI - Voice, Style, and Manuscript Rules Bible

## Point of View

- Deep third-person limited entirely through Adam.
- Past tense.
- No omniscient MH370 information.
- No information Adam could not perceive, remember, infer, or imagine.

## Prose Style

- Literary but accessible.
- Emotionally restrained.
- Behaviour before explanation.
- Sensory grounding before abstraction.
- No melodrama.
- No authorial winks.
- No "little did he know" or "last time" language.
- No explanation of symbolism.

## Malaysian Identity

Malaysian identity should feel lived-in and natural, not explained for an outside audience.

Use selective Malay where appropriate. Do not overload the prose with translation or cultural annotation unless Adam would naturally think that way.

## Islamic Texture

Islamic vocabulary should be quiet and authentic.

Faith appears as habit, memory, family rhythm, prayer, restraint, and moral reckoning. Avoid sermon-like exposition.

## Dream and Reality Rules

Dream typography must be identical to waking prose.

The dream/reality escalation should grow by part and must remain emotionally purposeful. Ambiguity may increase, but confusion should not replace meaning.

## Motif Control

Motifs must be used with discipline. Avoid overusing:

- later;
- home;
- tomorrow;
- stars;
- return;
- light;
- prayer;
- watches/time;
- messages/voice.

Motifs should accumulate through context rather than announcement.

## Prohibited Moves

- Do not solve MH370.
- Do not invent historical certainty.
- Do not create new conspiracy logic.
- Do not imply unsupported cause, route, cockpit event, passenger behaviour, crash, rescue, or afterlife.
- Do not introduce unnecessary new characters or subplots.
- Do not make fictional characters resemble specific real passengers or crew.
- Do not use the real tragedy for spectacle.

## Chapter Output Rule

Each drafting prompt should produce a complete polished manuscript chapter, not an outline, summary, or commentary.

After the chapter, include only a short Continuity Check confirming:

- frozen architecture preserved;
- historical boundary preserved;
- character continuity preserved;
- emotional objective achieved;
- any genuine unresolved continuity issue discovered.

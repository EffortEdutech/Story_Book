## Source Limitation

The referenced ChatGPT conversation reader exposed only the latest chapter-completion turns and did not provide an older-turn cursor. This binder therefore extracts and arranges the available local planning material plus the visible conversation state. If a full export of the original conversation is added later, these files can be reconciled against it.
